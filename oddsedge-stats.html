🛠️ How it works (No API)

  1. Enter team names below.
  2. Paste each team's recent fixtures (any league) or upload a CSV. We parse date, home, away, score.
  3. Optionally paste a combined H2H block or let us infer H2H from both feeds.
  4. Click Analyze. We'll compute form, averages, clean sheets, draw rate, H2H, and heuristic insights.
